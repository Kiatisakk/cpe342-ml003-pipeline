How Your Kaggle Score Converts to a Grade
Kaggle Score
Above Baseline
(Guarantee grade at α%)
Grade% = α + [ (100%-α) x Φ(t) ]
Where:
T-Score: t = (Your Score - μ) / σ
μ = Mean of scores above baseline
σ = Standard deviation of scores above baseline
Φ(t) = Standard Normal CDF (use Z-table)
μ
Baseline
designed to be achievable by everyone
Below Baseline
Grade% = α x (Your Score/Baseline)
Example Grading (α = 60%)
